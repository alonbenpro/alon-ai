## Design and scope

Use the existing SQLAlchemy/PostgreSQL migration chain and governed execution. Product experiment/workflow/agent rows extend the same governance IDs. Reuse immutable typed artifact storage for narrative plans, reports, proposals and bundles; keep ownership, inputs, current authority, decisions, contact protection, qualification and cardinality in relational columns and links. JSON must not hide an authoritative foreign key or bypass a database invariant.

Do not introduce an event-sourcing framework, another accounting ledger, another supply counter, per-agent databases or live provider/workflow implementations. L03 implements lifecycle persistence and deterministic acceptance commands; later tasks execute the live workflows. Preserve L02 contracts and tests. Existing accepted records remain immutable and inspectable under their source rights.

## Implementation slices

1. **Product roots and idea/research lineage.** Add `backend/src/alon_ai/records/` with strict versioned models, metadata and transactional commands; migration `20260912_04_product_records.py`; focused `test_product_records.py`. Enforce one current accepted idea and committed verdict per cycle, unapproved-pivot denial, immutable history and exact experiment scope. 2. **Offers and qualified campaign records.** Add bounded offer and lead modules and migration(s). Enforce exact accepted research input bundles and field-level commercial lineage, current PROCEED verdict, stale-proposal invalidation, one targeted offer-research return, one calibration, offer/profile/policy matching and normalized qualification criteria. Add global organization/domain/alias/location identities, independently licensed provenance, confirmed-contact/quarantine/reservation state, experiment candidate/contact/address/business-match records, complete evidence matrices and authoritative qualifications. Bind existing supply/contact IDs instead of duplicating their controls. Freeze a cohort only with exactly 50 current, protected, qualified/contactable members under the same accepted offer/profile. Working-list queries exclude all protected/nonqualified records without erasing history.
3. **Outreach and conversation records.** Add exact context/coverage/angle/narrative/strategy/sequence/draft lineage; enforce complete coverage, sensitive-fact exclusion, primary/supporting angle and CTA bounds, contact/greeting agreement and first-email disclosure restrictions. Persist conversations, messages/turns, untrusted-input safety findings, exact evidence spans/fact ledger, deterministic commercial/stage/response decisions, and versioned validated drafts. Enforce one active automation lock, manual-takeover and scheduling-only limits, unique invoice/demo actions, meeting handoff only after confirmed booking, operator messages/manual outcomes and checkpoints.
4. **Learning records and integrated acceptance.** Persist immutable strategy packages and member configurations, experiment freezes, learning inputs/scopes/proposals/candidates/comparisons, promotion/live observations/regression/rollback, rejected-version evidence and operator controls. Configuration schemas exclude protected policy; engineering requests cannot become executable strategy. Promotions apply to future work only, with exact candidate/baseline/evaluation lineage. Add one reloadable lifecycle fixture across all slices, update the structural schema manifest and concise implementation evidence.

These are maximums, not minimum legal retention. Accounting invoices may require a different period; `cost_entries.provider_invoice_ref` and finance evidence must receive an explicit counsel/accountant schedule without extending provider prompts, recipient bodies, tokens, sessions, or backup recovery points. Until real-data policy approval, use synthetic/owned aliases only. A missing duration blocks collection/send and automated purge job activation; it does not justify indefinite raw retention.

Purge exception state is exact. A batch that cannot prove deletion because of a dependency, ambiguous external receipt, or newly observed policy/hold mismatch enters `DEFERRED_REVIEW`, retains data without partial mutation, alerts the owner, and receives owner review within 24 hours of deferral. It must be resolved safely or escalated to counsel/security/incident ownership within 72 hours of deferral; neither SLA authorizes deletion or hold release. Missed 24/72-hour deadlines disable the affected provider/capability and both send controls where personal/outreach data is involved. Signed timestamps, owner, reason, evidence, next action, and escalation are retained; no silent indefinite queue exists.

### Holds, purge/redaction, and restored data

Active suppression may retain the deterministic SHA-256 recipient hash after contact/address deletion only for the counsel-approved safety hold. It remains pseudonymous/offline enumerable, column-restricted, purpose/query-rate/audit/alert controlled, absent from API/log/event/report/export, and protected by encrypted volume/WAL/snapshot/backups. Hash access anomaly opens `RECIPIENT_HASH_ENUMERATION` through IR-04. The current SHA-256 lookup is an explicit residual risk for this roadmap revision, not anonymization.

Hold precedence for live/product records is legal/regulator, incident/security, unresolved provider/send, active suppression/opt-out, rights dispute, decision/gate, dependency/reference, then normal expiry. Holds have exact target scope, reason enum, authority/reference, created/expiry/review, and immutable audit. They cannot be silently permanent; expired holds require review, not automatic release when risk remains. Backup/session/token maximums above are non-extendable overlays: before their deadline, the hold owner extracts only the minimum necessary evidence into the applicable restricted live record, then the general backup/session/token material expires normally.

`RetentionCommandService` plans a purge graph from the DB-06 FK manifest, snapshots row/count/hash references, redacts restricted payload/object first, inserts deletion evidence/tombstone, then purges only after all downstream references can preserve the minimum. Batches are idempotent and cursor/version bound. Active suppression persists as a recipient hash even after contact/address deletion. Domain/audit/send/repair history is never rewritten to pretend an action did not occur.

Backups are immutable-by-application-contract but bounded: deletion is recorded in live truth and applied immediately after restore before workers/read access, while lifecycle/prune enforces the registered retention ceiling over the single application-encrypted R2 repository and its WAL dependencies. Signed retention/deletion/tombstone evidence is application-owned and may outlive a backup chain only when its schema/content scan proves it contains no ciphertext, address, recipient hash, source locator, body, token, session handle/digest, or other reconstructible PII. Provider Object Lock or a second repository is not a pre-revenue prerequisite. Every restore runs the full authoritative tombstone replay, forces controls false, expires flows/revokes sessions, and validates no purged secret/payload becomes operationally accessible before API/workers/public ingress.

### Rights and verified operator procedure

Receipt channel is published in the privacy notice but does not become an unauthenticated product endpoint. Alon logs a local `DataRightsRequestV1`, verifies requester identity using the minimum appropriate evidence and a channel independent of the disputed address where proportionate, scopes jurisdiction/request without overcollection, and records deadlines/counsel reference. Unknown or impersonated requests expose nothing.

Search uses exact identity mappings and address hashes in a restricted process. Export is encrypted, time-limited, access-logged, and reviewed to exclude other persons, internal security secrets, privileged material, and provider data not authorized for disclosure. Correction creates a new version/event where records are mutable by design; immutable history is annotated/superseded, not overwritten. Deletion/objection invokes suppression first when marketing is involved, then retention redaction/purge under holds. Completion evidence contains counts/classes/hash/timestamps/reasons, not a new copy of the data. Counsel decides applicability, identity standard, exceptions, deadlines, and regulator notifications.

### Autonomous-sales field inventory and learning boundary

[data.inventory.rules.v1](data-inventory-rules.v1.json) is the complete deterministic rule authority for field classification/access, with [DB-06 resolution semantics](../02-database/06-migrations-seeding-and-retention.md#field-access-purpose-deletion-and-backup-contract) and the [source contract checker](verify-data-inventory-contract.py). DataInventoryV1 is its generated one-row-per-physical-column/nested-leaf/external-object projection, not a future decision about purposes or readers. Exact table entries cover offer economics, identities/evidence, phased qualification, complete conversation and budget evidence, every calendar record, checkpoint partitions, immutable IO, strategy versions/activations/rollback and action attribution. Ordered profiles resolve even an unrecognized leaf restrictively; an unknown table, schema hash mismatch or missing nested schema blocks collection. Each field row includes every privacy axis and exact owners; generic grouped owners cannot substitute for the table policy.

Normal writers and internal readers are exact service names; a mutable-field arm selects one owner from immutable direction/field predicates, never multiple competing writers. RetentionCommandService is the only purge/redaction writer. AgentInputMaterializer has no writes: it applies the exact named field transform and consumer allowlist and delegates immutable snapshot persistence. All model input is transform-only; global evaluation/learning receives only CHECKPOINT_LEARNING_V1 numeric/bounded-category cells with minimum cell size 5 and closed-checkpoint lineage. TYPED_ARTIFACT_INPUT_V1 recursively intersects upstream grants and cannot launder raw content into global access.

Descendant inheritance is a closed schema-checked intersection, never a container or prefix grant. data.inventory.rules.v1 fixes exact permissible metric, offer scope/deliverable/payment/timing leaf paths, types and descendant-safe policies. Both schema version/hash and accepted source schema/content binding are required; unknown/prohibited leaves, wrong types/hashes and whole containers deny. Numeric closed-checkpoint cells may reach the checkpoint transform under minimum cell 5; accepted offer terms may reach only offer consumers after redaction. Encrypted input/artifact wrappers carry the intersection of each normalized immutable source leaf's existing transform/consumer permissions, not a blanket read of snapshot_ciphertext.

SuppressionQueryService's internal uncached locked recipient_hash lookup is explicitly allowed by suppression_entries.internal_lookup_contract. query_readers describes an output port, not a reason to remove a datastore reader. The independent LOOKUP query profile denies hash serialization in every HTTP/report/export response and denies model/telemetry access. Only match status, safe reason and existing opaque target refs leave the lookup port.

Named transforms are deterministic allowlist projections with the exact dictionary/pattern/span algorithm in the rule file, not model privacy decisions. They drop unlisted structured fields, sensitive inference/budget assertion/private-calendar/credential fields, replace same-scope known contact/person/secret values longest-first, then mask every fixed-pattern or explicit restricted span. Invalid Unicode, missing source-scope/terms/hash, unknown schema or an invalid source-span map denies the affected value. Operational free text is permitted only through SANITIZED_THREAD_V1, accepted offer terms or approved business evidence selectors after this deterministic redaction; raw conversation body columns have no model grant. Global transforms drop every free-text field regardless of redaction. The transform hash, source schema/hash, consumer, allowed paths and expiry bind each input snapshot. No routine operator approval or model privacy certification is introduced.

Counsel may approve or shorten the declared duration maxima at the live gate. No later implementation discretion may widen purpose, sensitivity, readers/writers, encryption/redaction, model/telemetry access, holds, backup expiry or restore behavior; such a change needs a new versioned contract and full coverage proof.

Complete operational conversations are encrypted and available only while necessary to the authorized conversation/recovery purpose. Writer/evaluator calls receive complete sanitized context, exact accepted offer/strategy and permitted facts; personal addresses, unrelated thread content, private calendar details, credentials and sensitive unnecessary inferences are removed before a model call. Retaining raw and sanitized copies requires separate justified inventory rows, not a generic “agent memory” exception.

Global learning is permitted only over approved EvidenceTransformV1 outputs from closed checkpoints. The transform schema binds source IDs/versions/cutoff, primary/secondary/guardrail role, allowed non-PII fields, aggregation/minimum-cell rules, redaction version, output hash, permitted consumers and expiry. Raw PII, contact hashes, full thread bodies, calendar descriptions/attendees and sensitive inferred budget/attributes are excluded. A research or conversation access grant is not global-learning permission. Small groups/reidentifying combinations fail minimization even if each individual field seems harmless.

Promoted strategy/evaluation metadata may outlive sensitive payloads under EVALUATION_VERSIONED/SAFETY_LONG; its source text cannot inherit that longer clock. An expired snapshot/citation is shown as unavailable with retained hash/deletion evidence, never reconstructed from another campaign's raw text or backups. Source correction/deletion propagates to raw/sanitized/IO/cache/provider copies and dependent transform availability; immutable history records that deletion without fabricating missing evidence.

Qualified-call booking records buying intent and call agreement separately from optional purchase acceptance. Only exact confirmation source spans and authorized timezone details are stored for scheduling. Calendar descriptions contain minimized business context and never hidden prompts/budget inferences. Booking ambiguity holds the minimum action/provider evidence, not an unlimited full calendar archive.

Every new representation uses the same canonical retention.policy.v1 maximums and non-extendable AUTH_RUNTIME_DETAIL/PUBLIC_UNSUBSCRIBE_TOKEN/PUBLIC_UNSUBSCRIBE_KEY_RING/OPERATIONAL_BACKUP_CHAINS rules above. Deletion plans include independently field-encrypted copies, active provider stores, sanitized model snapshots and 35-day backup expiry. Restore reapplies tombstones before any read/worker/gateway, forces Gmail/calendar/scoped controls false and preserves suppression minimum/opaque target refs plus immutable strategy attribution.

## Security, privacy, compliance, idempotency, observability, and cost

Privacy is enforced by minimization, encryption, purpose/access allowlists, provider review, versioned retention and audited rights—not a legal-compliance claim. Purge/rights commands are authenticated, expected-versioned and idempotent. Telemetry records class/count/duration/outcome/reason, never target identity. Storage/KMS/egress/provider/legal costs are recorded without retaining personal data merely to explain cost.

## Failure, rollback, and operator recovery

On unknown purpose/retention, hold conflict, partial purge, identity doubt, reappearing restored data, provider exit failure, or leak: stop affected processing/provider/send, preserve minimal evidence, create incident, and seek counsel. Roll back code/policy for future jobs but never restore deleted payload into live use. Resume only after invariant/count/hash and rights/hold review; direct SQL deletion and manual object removal without tombstone are forbidden.

## Acceptance and retained evidence

- [ ] Every table/object/signal/prompt/provider/backup has purpose, class, source, owner, access, region/transfer, retention and deletion behavior.
- [ ] All DB-06 table classes/sole writer remain exact and provisional durations are replaced/approved before real data.
- [ ] Rights, holds, suppression minimum, immutable correction, purge, backup expiry and restored deletion are executable.
- [ ] Official rule, product policy, technical control, evidence, and counsel decision remain separate; no compliance claim is made.

Retain inventory/purpose/transfer/notice/retention versions, official sources/access dates, counsel/accountant references, access/purge/hold/tombstone manifests, rights verification/export/deletion evidence, provider exit proofs, canary scans, backup-expiry/restore reports, and privacy incident exercises.

## Outcome and timing

M9 phase 7 admits one pre-registered experiment/offer/campaign/mailbox/jurisdiction/legal-policy program only after phases 1–6 and M8 operational exit pass. Every capability remains planned today. The objective is evidenced qualified purchase commitments and confirmed qualified calls, with truthful claims, recipient choice, commercial floors and sender reputation. Payment collection is outside version one.

The operator signs pre-run scope, protected legal/provider evidence, source allowlist, budgets, controls and commercial envelope. Routine accepted artifacts, in-envelope drafts/replies/negotiations/checkpoints and confirmed bookings use deterministic owners through fresh action checks. The first controlled campaign can be supervised closely; observation is not a second send-authority path. Unsafe, ambiguous, stale or out-of-envelope cases pause into the exception/incident queue.

## Entry and exact four-cohort program

Entry binds source/release/runtime/schema/OpenAPI/catalog/retention hashes; current M0–M8 and six-phase evidence; approved agent baseline, accepted IdeaBrief/MarketResearchReport/OfferPackage/economics/evidence; global strategy/activation; one campaign/mailbox/calendar/credential generation; legal-policy/jurisdiction/disclosure/Google/source review; registered subsegment/causal/metric/evidence/cost definitions; budgets/windows/limits; current control/checkpoint generations; independent backup/restore/AWS witness/telemetry; public route-mode proof; operator signature, start and expiry. It contains safe opaque references, not recipient hashes/addresses, tokens, content or privileged legal text.

The four increments are 100/200/300/400 with cumulative maxima 100/300/600/1,000. Only the current stage obtains a server-owned eligibility/membership snapshot, immutable sorted set/hash and remaining-capacity reservation. Legal, provider, reputation, budget and configured bounds may admit fewer recipients; the numeric plan cannot override a smaller cap or turn underpowered evidence into CONTINUE. Recipient identity is unique across stages. Additional messages consume message/frequency/budget limits, not another recipient slot. No browser/agent preselects future members.

The first real campaign uses the stricter consent-only SEC-04 path: current affirmative consent with exact sender/purpose/channel/scope, independently supported identity/jurisdiction and current legal/Google/disclosure evidence. The COUNSEL_EXCEPTION route remains unavailable for this initial campaign. Research/qualification alone is not recipient legal authority. Fresh runtime records, never M3 fixture artifacts, are operands at each action.

## Bounded live conversation and booking

CampaignAdmissionService validates FINAL qualification, identity, suppression, current source/legal/offer/strategy/capacity before admission. ActionAuthorizationService creates immutable ActionAuthorityScopeV1 over exact action/content/recipient/thread/offer/strategy/activation/cohort/policy/commercial/expiry/generation. SendGateway alone consumes authority, freshly rebuilds all final facts and reserves rate/budget before its durable attempt and Gmail write. Intent/attempt/result/observation and idempotency are immutable; zero history/search observations never permit blind retry.

Any inbound reply atomically stops cold sequence admission before classification. Positive replies, questions and genuine objections may enter the bounded reply loop. Clear rejection closes persuasion; opt-out, complaint, hard bounce, soft-bounce limit or legal stop creates durable suppression only with the exact independent trigger evidence. Negative sentiment/ambiguous identity/unsafe intent pauses as policy requires. A terminal conversation cannot be automatically reopened.

CommercialPolicyEngine permits only supported explanation/objection answers, approved variants/pilots/discount bands, timing/bundle/payment changes, missing-information questions or call proposals inside accepted OfferPackage. Exact taxes, fees, delivery cost, FX, rounding, minimum net price and contribution-margin floor govern every proposal. STATED/INFERRED/UNKNOWN budget never collapses into inferred permission. Unsupported facts/guarantees/urgency/familiarity/deliverables/legal terms block; rounds/messages/frequency/deadline remain finite.

The purchase path includes INTERESTED→NEGOTIATING→COMMITTED→BOOKED; COMMITTED needs explicit purchase-proposal acceptance. Qualified INTERESTED/NEGOTIATING prospects may agree to a call and book without purchase acceptance; call agreement never counts as a purchase commitment. BookingGateway alone owns Google Calendar create/reschedule/cancel after explicit exact slot/timezone confirmation, fresh identity/context/availability/notification/controls and durable idempotent action. Ambiguity reconciles before any retry; booking evidence links business/contact/campaign/conversation/offer/strategy/activation without leaking calendar content.

## Automatic checkpoint and global learning

At each registered observation-window end, CheckpointEvaluationService closes admission, freezes CheckpointEvidenceBundle cutoff/member/denominator/evidence/cost versions and records exactly CONTINUE, REVISE, KILL, INCONCLUSIVE or SAFETY_STOP after deterministic rules. Incomplete/weak evidence and missing cost/FX/operator-time cannot become positive economics or continuation. Model confidence cannot override safety or missing data. Late observations append without mutating the frozen bundle.

Only CONTINUE at stages 1–3 can make the next registered cohort eligible; final CONTINUE at cumulative 1,000 records a positive terminal checkpoint without a fifth cohort or a new program. The closed checkpoint automatically triggers GlobalLearningEngine with the new stage primary, similar campaigns secondary and relevant history/failures/incidents as guardrails. Every applicable agent receives PROMOTE, KEEP, ROLLBACK or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; the latter two no-change cases KEEP and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE mutate nothing.

StrategyActivationService requires immutable lineage, minimum evidence, offline baseline comparison, protected holdout/transfer guardrails, expected metrics/confidence and stored rollback rules. Trigger campaign activation waits for its next cohort boundary after CONTINUE; other active campaigns wait for their own checkpoint; future campaigns use newest approved baseline. No offer/strategy/qualification/causal/evidence definition mutates mid-cohort. Deterioration immediately blocks affected actions, closes the paused checkpoint and applies compatible rollback at a boundary without rewriting history.

### Public unsubscribe route-mode truth table


An experiment abort always immediately commits `PRODUCT_OUTREACH=false`, closes/cancels provably unsent intent and work through canonical command authority, stops new admission/dequeue/provider construction, and keeps every sales agent, scheduler, worker and send/booking write authority off. Abort does **not** by itself remove an opt-out mechanism from recipients who already received a message. The signed route-mode record binds the exact release/policy/token-key/cohort, expected route/control versions, active-token expiry envelope, public/suppression/witness/telemetry health checks, operator/counsel decision, UTC and evidence hashes.

| Route mode | Entry condition | Exact reachable surface and authority | Required operator action and evidence | Exit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ACTIVE` | M9 entry is open, the exact public/suppression/witness/telemetry dependencies are healthy and the experiment has not aborted | exactly the scanner-safe GET and explicit POST; private/product/send routes remain unreachable publicly; `PRODUCT_OUTREACH` is a separate versioned fact and no public call can authorize send | continuously prove exact-private/2-public set equality, GET write-zero, POST atomic suppression, token/replay/WAF/redaction and dependency health | normal close or any abort commits product off and re-evaluates into `SUPPRESSION_ONLY` or `DISABLED_UNSAFE` |
| `SUPPRESSION_ONLY` | experiment closed/aborted while an active token, policy or delivered-recipient obligation remains, and the edge plus `RecipientSignalSuppressionService`, PostgreSQL, token/replay, witness and telemetry dependencies are all proven healthy | retain exactly the same GET/POST pair in an isolated suppression-only deployment; every agent/worker/send/private/product route and `PRODUCT_OUTREACH` stay off | keep health/route/suppression evidence current for the full obligation, process POST idempotently, monitor the mailbox reply opt-out path manually, reconcile signals/backlog and retain daily cohort/token/route evidence | restore `ACTIVE` only for a separately valid non-aborted entry; otherwise retire the deployment only after the signed retirement criteria below |
| `DISABLED_UNSAFE` | any public/suppression/token/DB/WAF/witness/telemetry dependency is unsafe or its truth is unknown | pair is immediately unpublished; no public route remains and all product/send/agent/worker authority stays off | proactively create active recipient suppression for **every delivered cohort member** through canonical `SuppressionCommandService` authority using opaque member refs; preserve and manually monitor reply-based opt-out plus the exact counsel-approved alternate channel; page `ALERT_COMPLIANCE_SUPPRESSION`, open `COMPLIANCE_OR_SUPPRESSION_BREACH` under `IR-12`, notify counsel and record the counsel decision; retain DNS/WAF/route/backlog/cohort/suppression set-equality evidence | only `SUPPRESSION_ONLY`, never `ACTIVE`, after exact recovery, backlog replay and operator/counsel sign-off; product remains false |

Restoration from `DISABLED_UNSAFE` requires a clean immutable release/config; exact `T7-PUBLIC-EDGE-VERIFY` exit `0`; private-plus-two-public route set equality; scanner GET write-zero; POST Origin/CSRF/content-type/token/replay/WAF/redaction and serializable suppression tests; dependency-failure and repair tests; complete replay of every queued public/reply/alternate-channel observation; delivered-cohort-to-active-suppression set equality for the outage interval; zero no-next-`SEND` violations; current witness/telemetry; closed or explicitly contained `IR-12`; and signed operator/counsel evidence. Re-publication enters `SUPPRESSION_ONLY` and cannot revive the aborted campaign.

Safe retirement is not `DISABLED_UNSAFE`: after all active tokens have expired or been fulfilled, the full counsel-defined policy and delivered-recipient obligation has ended, all observations/backlogs are terminal, no route/suppression incident remains and counsel/operator sign the evidence, the isolated pair may be unpublished and its route-mode record closed. Retirement never deactivates suppression or deletes token, signal, incident or recipient-obligation evidence.


## Abort, re-entry and evidence

Immediately stop affected admission/actions, and the entire campaign where SEC-04 requires, on unauthorized/wrong-identity/duplicate/provider-ambiguous effects, suppression uncertainty, unsupported commercial commitment/floor breach, control/cap/cost violation, unsafe source/legal/provider drift, privacy incident, unknown audit/witness/backup/telemetry truth or unsafe public suppression dependency. Preserve possibly-called send/booking attempts and current reservations. A complaint/hard bounce follows the stricter SEC-04 stop regardless of demand. No compensating unchecked calendar cancel or replacement message is permitted.

After abort, reconcile provider truth, resolve the exact incident/root cause and legal obligations, revalidate affected evidence and create a separately authorized equal-or-smaller entry/version where required; never revive an aborted cohort. Maintain suppression-only public service while obligations remain and dependencies are healthy. Re-enable requires fresh typed gate evidence and never erases suppression/history. A positive checkpoint cannot authorize another program, market, source/legal policy, cap increase or payment collection.

Retain action/offer/strategy/activation/cohort/checkpoint hashes, exact unique denominators, accepted facts/claims/qualification, reply/negotiation/booking outcomes, costs/operator time, incidents, route-mode/control evidence and all learning/rollback results. Synthetic/owned-alias records are excluded from real demand.

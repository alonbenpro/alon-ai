## Outcome and timing

Earned autonomy means bounded deterministic execution of the accepted sales program. It allows routine in-envelope writing, sending, replying, negotiation, explicitly confirmed booking and checkpoint/global learning after the complete evidence ladder passes. Agents propose artifacts/actions; deterministic services own acceptance, policy, admission, writes and activation. This roadmap implements no runtime and grants no real outreach authority.

The exact eight-phase launch ladder is ordered and cannot be skipped:

| Phase | Evidence gate | Exact roadmap provider / exit |

| 1 | synthetic agent pipeline | WF-03-T05, WF-04-T05 and AGENT-10-T05: both idea origins, provider-before-consumer and all accepted artifacts/qualification with no real send |
| 2 | recorded provider fixtures | TEST-02-T04, TEST-04-T05 and PROVIDER-07-T04: typed Gmail/calendar/read/model unions, deterministic policy and crash/ambiguity fixtures |

## Phase-8 eligibility and frozen execution envelope

The phase-8 entry decision binds the exact proven campaign/program, offer version/hash/economics, source/legal/provider policy, agent baseline/configuration, GlobalStrategyPackage/StrategyActivation, cohort/checkpoint generations, calendar/mailbox identity, action/control scopes, metrics/cost definitions, finite round/message/frequency/window/provider/cash/time caps and rollback target. It consumes current phase-7 outcome, retained incident-free or resolved evidence, full M0–M8/M9 gates and complete commercial/deliverability/recovery proof. Unavailable, stale, underpowered or safety-stopped evidence does not earn a broader capability.

Pre-register chronological eligible/admitted populations, windows, required sample sizes, metric/rule versions, source/segment allocation and exclusions before execution. Include every admitted outcome, billed failure, abstention, timeout, cancellation and unresolved action; only evidenced pre-admission rejection stays outside the admitted denominator. Missing labels/cost/attendance remain unknown. No cherry-picked successful-run IDs, denominator resets, retrospective threshold changes or synthetic evidence as demand.

Stage 8 may follow successful controlled observation at an eligible closed checkpoint under the same remaining registered program; it cannot add a recipient or alter the offer/strategy halfway through a running cohort. If phase 7 has already reached the 1,000 ceiling, earned eligibility is recorded for future separately authorized programs and creates no additional admission. A fresh program still needs explicit scope/source/legal/budget authority.

## What bounded execution permits

| Responsibility | Agent role | Deterministic owner and fixed bound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| discovery/research/offer | accepted ordered proposals using approved sources; optional manual idea only | accepted immutable IdeaBrief→MarketResearchReport→OfferPackage; BusinessIdentityService/QualificationService and current source envelope |
| initial and reply writing | evidence-backed EmailDraft with sanctioned objective/full sanitized thread | ActionAuthorizationService and fresh SendGateway checks; writer never receives Gmail credentials or write port |
| negotiation | explain/answer objection/select approved variant/pilot/discount/timing/bundle/payment/call objective | CommercialPolicyEngine stored economics; minimum price/margin, proof, STATED budget and conversation limits cannot change |
| confirmed call booking | qualified buying intent and explicit call agreement/slot evidence | BookingGateway alone creates/reschedules/cancels; fresh identity/availability/timezone/notification/policy and ambiguity reconciliation |
| checkpoint | evaluate frozen evidence and recommend | CheckpointEvaluationService exactly CONTINUE/REVISE/KILL/INCONCLUSIVE/SAFETY_STOP, increments 100/200/300/400, cumulative 100/300/600/1,000 |
| global learning | analyze triggering primary, similar secondary and historical guardrail evidence for every applicable agent | StrategyActivationService gates PROMOTE/KEEP/ROLLBACK/INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, protected holdouts/transfer and boundary-only activations |

KEEP and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE change nothing. The triggering campaign uses a promoted strategy only at its next cohort boundary after CONTINUE; other active campaigns wait for their own checkpoint; future campaigns start with newest approved global version. An active cohort freezes offer, strategy, qualification rules, causal variables and evidence definitions. Every call/policy/draft/send/negotiation/booking/checkpoint/learning result retains exact governing strategy and activation. Operational thread memory is not a second learning mechanism.

Explicit rejection closes persuasion; ordinary replies stop cold sequence without inventing durable suppression. Only qualifying opt-out/complaint/bounce-limit/legal evidence creates durable suppression. BOOKED terminates sales outreach. Qualified call agreement can lead to booking without purchase acceptance and never inflates COMMITTED or purchase commitment metrics.

## Boundaries, deterioration and rollback

Autonomy never grants agents/provider wrappers direct Gmail/calendar writes, credentials, arbitrary sources/network, fabricated claims/urgency/budget, unsupported guarantees/legal terms, below-floor pricing, uncontrolled prompt rewriting, suppression deactivation, budget/cap increase, public-route changes, release/restore cutover, incident closure, payment collection or a new experiment/program. Operator exception resolution cannot waive immutable limits. CONTINUE at 1,000 is terminal and authorizes no fifth cohort.

Apply stored hard-safety, cost, latency, quality/transfer and reputation deterioration rules to complete chronological action populations. Immediately block affected future actions; pause any active cohort and close its checkpoint; then StrategyActivationService validates and applies a prior compatible approved package at that boundary. Keep action/result/checkpoint attribution immutable. An unavailable or incompatible rollback remains blocked and opens the exact exception/incident. Removing a kill switch or re-enabling a release is a separate protected command with fresh evidence.

A tighter limit can stop execution immediately; it cannot rewrite frozen commercial/evidence definitions for continuation. Recovery must preserve current offer/strategy/cohort/checkpoint/conversation/booking/suppression state and reconcile every possibly-called provider action. Return to the first failed evidence phase for the affected capability, retain first failure and rerun complete relevant tests; do not average away a hard failure or treat time alone as earned permission.

## Test strategy, acceptance and recovery

- [ ] Every required positive/negative/concurrent case has a fixture identity, versioned owner, command, evidence hash and fail action; missing or unavailable evidence is not passing.
- [ ] Only SendGateway invokes Gmail writes and only BookingGateway invokes calendar create/reschedule/cancel. Crash/replay cannot duplicate effects.
- [ ] Cohorts remain 100/200/300/400 with cumulative 100/300/600/1,000 and no active-cohort mutation or fifth cohort.
- [ ] Decision sets remain CONTINUE/REVISE/KILL/INCONCLUSIVE/SAFETY_STOP and PROMOTE/KEEP/ROLLBACK/INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; weak evidence cannot promote or continue.
- [ ] Retain signed case/result/command/fixture/schema/provider/strategy/activation hashes, call counts, costs, immutable action history and safe failure traces. Raw PII, message/calendar content, sensitive inferred attributes and credentials are excluded from ordinary telemetry/global learning.

Preserve first failures and resolve root cause; never average away safety failures or rewrite labels/history. Restore/rollback keeps admission off, applies current suppression/tombstones and reconciles possibly-called effects before any separately authorized re-entry.
